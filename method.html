01

Eight rules, each dated

What the engine does, and where each operation comes from.

Mūlāṅka
The birth day reduced to one digit. Born on the 29th: 2 + 9 = 11 → 2. Ordinary digital-root arithmetic.
Documented
Bhāgyāṅka
Every digit of the full date summed and reduced: 29-08-1995 → 43 → 7.
Documented
Nāmāṅka
Chaldean letter values, each word reduced and the words summed. John Smith: 9 + 8 = 17 → 8.
Documented
Digit to graha
Nine digits for nine planets. 4 is Rāhu and 7 is Ketu in the Indian form.
Documented
Lo Shu square
Every non-zero digit of the date, plus the bhāgyāṅka, plus the mūlāṅka when the birth day is ten or more.
Chinese
Kua
Both century branches: for births from 2000, men reduce 9 − Y, women 6 + Y.
Chinese
Vimśottarī daśā
From the Moon's sidereal longitude: the nakṣatra gives the lord, the fraction the balance.
Classical
The 45-year cycle
One year for 1, two for 2, to nine — summing to 45. Not a classical daśā.
