Rule 0

Digital reduction

Digits are summed until one remains. 1995 → 1+9+9+5 = 24 → 2+4 = 6. Zero is never a result: a sum of 0 becomes 9, because the system has nine numbers for nine grahas. Master numbers (11, 22, 33) are kept only when the toggle is on, and that convention is Pythagorean.

Cheiro, 1926

Compass school

Kua number

Let y be the last two digits of the birth year, summed to one digit.

  • 1900–1999 · male: 10 − y   · female: 5 + y
  • 2000–2099 · male: 9 − y   · female: 6 + y
  • if the result is 5 → 2 for male, 8 for female
  • east group: 1, 3, 4, 9  ·  west group: 2, 5, 6, 7, 8

Two branches are required. A single formula applied to both centuries misdirects every user born after 1999.

Modern

Timing cycles

  • personal year = reduce(day + month + current year)
  • personal month = reduce(personal year + current month)
  • personal day = reduce(personal month + current day)
  • aṅka cycle = 1 year for 1, 2 for 2 … 9 for 9, repeating every 45 years

Arithmetic only. These are not classical daśās, which are computed from the Moon’s longitude.

Piṅgala, c. 2nd c. BCE
