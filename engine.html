Compound numbers, 10–52

10Rise and fall in a single arc; the wheel turns twice.
11A warning number: hidden conflict, and strength found in resisting it.
12Sacrifice and enclosure; growth that needs a boundary.
13Change of plan, not of fortune; the old order breaks usefully.
14Movement, trade and communication; gain through variety.
15Eloquence and magnetism; the risk is persuasion without substance.
16A sudden collapse of a structure built on the wrong foundation.
17Immortality of the name; the classic number of the artist who outlives the age.
18Material struggle against spiritual insight; the sword and the mirror.
19The sun at noon: honour, and the danger of standing too high.
20Awakening after delay; a call to a purpose larger than the self.
21The crown won after a long contest; victory that must be maintained.
22The man in the cage: insight that is imprisoned by circumstance.
23The star of the lion: success through patronage and the help of others.
24The web of love and protection; a fortunate number for partnership.
25Strength won by experience rather than by gift.
26Counsel that profits others more than the giver.
27The sceptre: authority exercised through creative command.
28Promise that outruns the harvest; gain expected but not assured.
29Trials of the spirit, with unexpected help arriving late.
30Reflective contemplation; fortune follows thought, not action.
31The hermit: self-contained, and often solitary in success.
32Fellowship in numbers; fortune shared is fortune kept.
33The number of the teacher; knowledge that must be given away.
34The number of the sage, whose counsel outlives him.
35Depth of mind and long memory; the burden of carrying both.
36The wide circle; prosperity through many small loyalties.
37Love of the whole; friendship that becomes a fortune.
38The astral number: subtle forces, and gains that arrive quietly.
39The sceptic who is forced to believe; insight after resistance.
40The column: endurance, and a strength that does not bend.
41The magician: power over circumstance, and the duty that comes with it.
42The heart of the community; prosperity through service.
43Revolution from within; the old life ends to make the new one.
44The quiet builder; the same strength as 26 but turned outward.
45The eagle: far sight, and gain through long perspective.
46The great harvest; comfort won by effort over many years.
47The widow: strength discovered after loss.
48The judge: authority, and the cost of using it well.
49The long war; victory through persistence rather than force.
50The wheel of fortune; a number of turning points.
51The warrior: sudden advancement, and the enemies that come with it.
52The same as 43: a closing of one arc and the opening of the next.
