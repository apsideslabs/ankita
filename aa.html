System II · Axiom Arcana

A second engine, with its own mathematics.

Aṅkita’s first engine reads the classical and Chaldean tradition and needs two inputs. This one is different in kind, and it needs four: name, date, time and place, because twelve of its bindings read the time and place seeds. It is a numerology that borrows the shape of astrology and nothing else: reduce-to-13 instead of reduce-to-9, thirteen realms instead of twelve signs, a symmetric letter code instead of Chaldean values, a 91-year cycle instead of a daśā, and 27 stations reached by its own arithmetic rather than by an ephemeris.
