System IIThe AA System · Axiom Arcana

A second engine, with its own mathematics.

Aṅkita’s first engine reads the classical and Chaldean tradition and needs two inputs — a name and a date. This one is different in kind, and it needs four: name, date, time and place, because twelve of its bindings read the time and place seeds. The Axiom Arcana is an authored system — reduce-to-13 instead of reduce-to-9, thirteen realms instead of twelve signs, a symmetric letter code instead of Chaldean values, and a 91-year cycle instead of a daśā.

What is verified: every rule here is transcribed from the Codex and Research Document, and the test suite checks the engine against the worked examples printed in them — Reduce13(49) = 13, 3 March 2009 → Core Self 8, ANUJ → 3 · 7 · 5, PRIYA → 5 · 10 · 4. The Moon longitude is a truncated series, good to about half a degree; the console warns when the result sits near a station boundary.

ISix calculations

What the AA engine actually computes.

01

Reduce-to-13

The system’s fundamental reduction. Any integer collapses to 1–13 and stops at 13, which is a base number here rather than a pass-through. 49 → 13, not 4.

02

Core Self

Day reduced to 13, month taken as is, year’s digits summed and reduced. The three are added and reduced again — the centre of the 12-realm ring.

03

Axiomatic Letter Code

A symmetric pairing, A↔Z and B↔Y, so values mirror around the alphabet’s centre. Three name numbers: DPN, SWN, PMN.

04

27 Stations

The ecliptic divided into 27 equal stations of 13.333°, each quartered. The Moon’s sidereal longitude gives the station and the pada.

05

91-year APS

Nine forces in a repeating cycle — 7, 10, 7, 13, 13, 13, 13, 7, 8 — summing to 91. Birth begins mid-period, at the fraction of the station already traversed.

06

PLY · PLM · PLD

Personal lunar year, month and day, each a reduce-to-13 of the previous layer plus the calendar value.
