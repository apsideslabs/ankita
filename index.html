What the numbers are made of

Three traditions, named separately

Most numerology merges a 1926 London letter table, a 1990 Indian graha scheme and a Chinese number square, then calls the result ancient. Aṅkita keeps them apart and prints which one produced each figure.

1926 · London

Chaldean arithmetic

Letter values, the compound numbers 10–52, and single-digit reduction. Verified against Cheiro’s own worked example.

1990 · India

Graha-numerology

Nine digits for nine grahas, with Rāhu at 4 and Ketu at 7 — the substitution the Indian form makes to Cheiro’s Uranus and Neptune.

Chinese

The Lo Shu square

Placement, missing and repeated digits, plane completion. Labelled Chinese because that is what it is.

Inside the engine

Deterministic, and open to inspection

One pure function takes a name and a date and returns everything: three core numbers, the grid, the friendships, the timing cycles and the provenance list. No network, no model, no hidden step.

What the engine refuses to do

It will not print a figure whose rule it cannot name. The digit-to-graha pairing has no classical Sanskrit citation — none has been found — so the platform says so, in the interface, on the page where the pairing is used.
