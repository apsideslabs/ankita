About

Aṅkita

अङ्कित — “the numbered”, “that which is marked”

Aṅkita is the numerology member of the Apsides Labs family: Gaṇaka computes the chart, Phalita reads it, and Aṅkita reads the number. All three share one principle — a figure may be printed only if the rule behind it can be named.

Sourced, not asserted

Every rule carries a date and an author. Where a source is missing, the interface says so rather than filling the gap.

Three layers, named

Chaldean arithmetic, Indian graha-numerology and the Chinese Lo Shu grid are kept apart, because they are three different things.

Computed locally

The engine is a pure function in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and the same input always gives the same output.

What it will not do

  • · Claim a Vedic pedigree for a twentieth-century table.
  • · Present a Chinese grid or a Feng Shui number as Indian.
  • · Promise an outcome. The engine computes; the reading is yours.
  • · Hide a formula. The whole engine is in lib/engine.

Technical

  • · Next.js 15 · React 19 · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS v4.
  • · Engine: pure TypeScript, no dependencies, deterministic.
  • · Tests: npm test — the suite checks the engine against the sources’ own worked examples.
  • · Licence: MIT. Sources remain the property of their authors.
